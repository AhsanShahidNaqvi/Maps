About the data

What am I looking at?
Satellite imagery
Photographic basemap (Standard Satellite). Shows what the surface looks like.
Terrain-DEM
Numeric elevation surface (Mapbox Terrain-DEM). Drives 3D relief and elevation queries.

A visualization derived from the DEM to emphasize relief.
Geology
Separate supplied vector data. Empty here until verified GeoJSON is pasted into APP_CONFIG.

Hiding geology changes display visibility only — it does not remove physical terrain or beds.

3D Terrain & Geology Explorer

Murli / Mulri Hills

24.9197421° N, 67.1127104° E

An oblique, scroll-driven tour of the terrain around a single supplied map anchor, rendered from satellite imagery and a global elevation model.

Scroll the story cards. Drag to pan. Wheel/pinch to zoom. Right-drag or two-finger gesture to rotate and pitch.
